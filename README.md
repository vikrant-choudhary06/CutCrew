## What is CutCrew?

CutCrew is a streaming app for movies and TV series with the feel of the big OTT
platforms. Open it, pick something from the rows on the home screen or search for
it, and it plays — with your progress remembered, so you can pick up where you left off.

## Features

### Discover
- **Featured banner** that rotates through what's popular, with the title's own logo artwork
- **Top 10 movies** and **popular series** updated from what people are watching
- **New releases** and rows for every mood — action, comedy, thrillers, crime, animation, romance, horror and more
- **Browse by genre** for movies and series, with endless "load more"
- **Search** across movies and series, with recent searches remembered
- **More like this** suggestions on every title

### Watch
- **One-tap play** from the home screen or any title page
- **Resume where you left off** — on movies and on each episode of a series
- **Seasons and episodes** with thumbnails, names and your progress on each one
- **Next episode** countdown, so a series keeps going on its own
- **Instant titles** — popular titles are kept on CutCrew's own storage, marked with ⚡ and start faster
- **Automatic backup sources** — if one source fails, CutCrew moves to the next without you doing anything
- **Keyboard shortcuts** and double-tap to skip on the web player

### Your library
- **My List** to save movies and series for later
- **Continue watching** row with how much is left
- **Profiles** so everyone in the house keeps their own list and progress
- **Accounts** that keep your list and progress in sync on the website
